Protection Protocols

WE CARE FOR YOUR HOME AS IF IT WERE OUR OWN

Many clients fear walls being scratched or decor damaged by careless contractors. At Esparza's, we use active safety measures that ordinary cleaners overlook.

1. Wall Corner Guards

Steam extraction hoses are heavy and rigid. To prevent rubbing against fresh wall paint or chipping baseboards, we place specialized corner guards with smooth rollers at every hallway turn and doorway.

2. Caution Cones (Slippery Floor Warning)

Stepping from freshly cleaned, damp carpet onto tile or wood floors can cause shoe soles to slip. We set up bright yellow caution signs to keep children and seniors safe.

3. Respect for Furniture & Valuables

We place plastic pads or protective foam blocks under the legs of heavy wooden furniture to prevent wood stains from bleeding onto damp carpet while it dries. We treat every item with utmost care.

4. Full Commercial Liability Insurance

Your property is 100% protected. We maintain active commercial and residential general liability insurance for your peace of mind.
